# Appendix Section Specs

Specifications for the three appendix sections of a facilitation guide: Outcomes & Evidence of Success, Design Rules, and Facilitation Notes. Load this when building the appendix (Build Mode 1, step 6).

## Outcomes & Evidence of Success

Two categories, each in a table:

**Attitudinal Outcomes (Primary):**

| Outcome | Description | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Name | What the outcome means | Observable in-room evidence |

Typical attitudinal outcomes: Seeing opportunities, Feeling supported (not threatened), Willing to try.

**Skill-Based Outcomes (Vehicle for attitudinal shift):**

| Outcome | Description | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Name | What the participant can do | Observable/testable evidence |

Skill outcomes map 1:1 to activities. Each activity produces one skill outcome.

**The relationship:** Attitudinal outcomes are the primary goal. Skill outcomes are the vehicle. The activities teach skills, but the purpose is the attitudinal shift. Design decisions should optimize for attitudinal outcomes, using skill outcomes as the mechanism.

## Design Rules

A bullet list of principles that drove design decisions. Derived from evidence analysis: each rule should connect to what makes this workshop effective.

**Format:**

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Rule name** | One-sentence explanation |

**Common rules (adapt per workshop):**
- Structured reflection after every activity
- Target tedious tasks, not proud work
- Hands-on mechanics (not just demos)
- Participants articulate the insight (not us telling them)
- Bridge to a specific deadline (micro-commitment)
- Frame: AI for overhead, not for craft

---

## Facilitation Notes

Manager and stakeholder input organized into four categories:

**Team concerns:**
- Trust/fear issues, AI familiarity levels, buy-in status
- Specific concerns flagged by management

**Framing approach:**
- Frame language (e.g., "AI as friction remover, not replacement")
- Values to open with (editorial judgment, member trust, human oversight)
- Acknowledgment strategy for job concerns

**Language guidelines:**
- Empathy-first language preferences
- Phrases to use and avoid
- Preferred framing patterns

**Safety and change management:**
- Verification requirements (facts, hallucinations, stakeholder intent)
- Change management approach (small experiments first)
- Failure normalization strategy
