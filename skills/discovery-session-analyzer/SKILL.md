# Discovery Session Analyzer

## Overview
Transform meeting transcripts and discovery session notes into structured AI opportunity readouts with clear identification of information gaps for AI workshop planning.

## When to Use This Skill
Use this skill when you have:
- Raw meeting transcripts from discovery sessions
- Unstructured notes from team interviews
- Need to create formal AI opportunity readouts
- Want to identify information gaps for follow-up

## Input Format

Provide your discovery session materials in this format:

```
Team: [Team Name] ([Number] people)
Company: [Company Name] (optional)

Meeting Notes:
[Raw transcript or structured notes from discovery session including:]
- Team responsibilities and current processes
- Pain points and challenges
- Technology tools currently used
- Workflow descriptions
- Time estimates for tasks
- Quality requirements
- Examples of work products
```

## Process

I will read your notes three times to ensure comprehensive analysis:

1. **First Reading - Overall Comprehension**: Understanding team context, general workflows, and major pain points
2. **Second Reading - Nuance and Details**: Capturing specific tasks, technology details, and quality requirements
3. **Third Reading - Final Extraction**: Verifying all content and identifying opportunities

## Output Structure

I will generate a structured discovery readout following this format:

### AI Discovery Readout
- **Team Summary**: Overview, challenges, success factors, technology ecosystem
- **Level 1 Opportunities**: Single-step AI tasks organized by type (Draft & Refine, Distill, Lookup, Brainstorm, Research)
- **Level 2 Opportunities**: Complex multi-step workflows for deep dive workshops
- **Implementation Considerations**: Team readiness, strategic priority, technical prerequisites
- **Information Gaps Summary**: Clear list of missing information requiring follow-up

## Key Principles

- **Extract Only**: Use only information explicitly found in source material
- **Mark Gaps Clearly**: Use "Unknown" for any missing information
- **Maintain Structure**: Follow exact output format regardless of available information
- **Document Gaps**: Provide clear summary of missing information for follow-up

## Example Usage

Simply paste your discovery session notes and I'll transform them into a professional AI opportunity readout that identifies both actionable opportunities and information gaps requiring follow-up questions.
