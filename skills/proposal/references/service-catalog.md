# Mellonhead Service Catalog

Reference this catalog when mapping client needs to services (proposal skill, Mode 3). Pricing here is directional; adjust based on scope, org size, and negotiation. The catalog is a starting menu, not a default order: composition is always derived from the brief.

## Curriculum: Foundation

| Module | Audience | Topics | Delivery |
|--------|----------|--------|----------|
| Foundation Core | All employees | Intro to AI, Company AI Policy & Compliance, Intro to Copilot (or other tool), Prompting 101 | Self-paced + live VILT |

## Role-Based Workshops

**Process:** Discovery (interview SMEs, map workflows, evaluate readiness) → Readout (prioritize activities by readiness and impact) → Development (build approach, tools, guidance from real artifacts) → Delivery (hands-on, practical workshops grounded in the team's actual tasks)

- Tailored to specific functions: HR, Marketing, Finance, Operations, Legal, etc.
- Cycle repeats for each new role
- Typically 1 role/month, can scale to 2-4 roles/month

## AI Champions Program

- Monthly cohort of ~24 power users (one per team, rotating terms)
- Three pillars: Ongoing Learning (sync + async, new features, advanced training), Peer Support & Guidance (prompting techniques, success stories, lessons learned), Amplify Success & Momentum (experimentation, team training, answering questions)
- Mellonhead leads monthly sessions and moderates the channel
- Client builds champion responsibilities into role expectations

## Foundation Cohort (mixed-proficiency teams)

- 12-month, role-based, for teams developing AI capability together
- 2hr monthly session + 1hr monthly demo day + office hours
- Intake/midpoint surveys, capstone + certification

## Leadership Intensive (introduced via {{client 2}}, May 2026)

- 3-month, three-session leadership-focused engagement
- For senior leaders (~30 people) at Lighthouse Clients
- Sessions: See What AI Can Really Do / Think Strategically About AI / Leading Through Change
- Optional add-on: AI Leadership Playbook (framework reference + Claude skills + 5 Manager Behaviors)

## Live Sessions

- Sold in blocks (e.g., 40 sessions)
- Delivered via client's video platform (typically Teams or Zoom)
- Max participants per session (typically 24)
- 2 facilitators per session
- All sessions recorded for reuse
