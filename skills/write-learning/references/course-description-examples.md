# Course Description Examples

Exemplar course descriptions referenced by the write-learning skill (Mode 4). Use these as calibration for length, structure, and voice.

## Catalog listing example (one paragraph)

> "The AI Academy Foundation program builds the knowledge and skills you need to use AI responsibly and effectively at ABA. Across six courses, you'll go from understanding what AI can do to using it confidently in your daily work, with the judgment to know when it's helping and when to step in. Complete the program at your own pace to earn the AI Foundation badge."

## Detailed listing example (course-level)

> "AI is already changing how work gets done in banking. This course cuts through the hype and gives you a clear picture of what generative AI can actually do, where it struggles, and how ABA is approaching it. You'll walk away knowing enough to have an informed opinion, not just a list of buzzwords."

## Blended course description set (Part 1 + Part 2)

Reference examples: Communicating with AI (Part 1 / Part 2) and Turning Data Into Stories with Copilot (Part 1 / Part 2 / whole course). The latter set:

> **Part 1 (self-paced).** Analyzing data, finding the story in it, and answering a business question is an important skill for many roles. The hard part is often that data pulled from different systems isn't ready to analyze the moment you export it, and handing it to Copilot to analyze outright gives you answers you can't fully trust.
>
> This self-paced course introduces a three-phase approach and what it looks like to coach Copilot through each phase instead of accepting its first answer. It sets up the hands-on practice in Part 2, where you'll work a real analysis from messy data to a finished readout.

> **Part 2 (hands-on).** Hand Copilot a business question and a pile of data, tell it to analyze, and you'll usually get something polished that doesn't hold up. It makes assumptions you never approved, glosses over problems buried in the data, and answers a slightly different question than the one you asked.
>
> This hands-on workshop teaches you to work through the analysis alongside Copilot instead of handing it off. Across three phases, you'll prepare messy data from different systems, confirm the methodology and validate what Copilot produces, and shape the analysis into a story your stakeholders can trust. You coach and check the work at each step rather than accepting the first answer.
>
> Building on the Part 1 course, you'll practice on a real dataset and business question that you bring.

> **Whole course.** If you have data and a business question, this course teaches you how to get from one to the other using Copilot, resulting in answers you can trust. Copilot can do much of that work with you, once you know how to guide it.
>
> The course teaches a three-phase approach: prepare the data, analyze it, then turn it into a story. You coach Copilot throughout and check its work at each phase rather than accepting its first answer.
>
> Part 1 is a self-paced course that introduces the approach. Part 2 is a hands-on workshop where you practice it end to end. Use a real dataset and business problem that you bring, or use a prepared example.
